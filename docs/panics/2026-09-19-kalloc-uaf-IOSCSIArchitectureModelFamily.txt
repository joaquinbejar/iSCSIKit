Why it matters regardless of cause
----------------------------------
DriverKit's purpose is that a userspace driver cannot corrupt kernel memory.
Whatever this dext does or fails to do, the kernel's SCSI stack should not
reach a use-after-free. The panic is inside
IOSCSIArchitectureModelFamily / IOSCSIBlockCommandsDevice, not in any
third-party kext; there are none loaded in the backtrace.

Not yet established
-------------------
- Which object the 288-byte allocation belongs to.
- Whether a completion from this dext (or its timing) triggers the free, or
  whether the failing wipe path leaves the SCSI layer in a bad state.
- Whether it reproduces. It has happened once.
